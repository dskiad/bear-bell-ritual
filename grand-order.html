Grand Order Structure

Grand Body

Grand Assembly

The Grand Assembly of the Most Honourable and Entirely Imaginary Order of the Bear Bell

Presided over by the Most Honourable Grand Chief Ranger.

Subordinate Body

Bear Bell Assembly

A local body may be styled Bear Bell Assembly No. ___ and be presided over by a Chief Ranger.

The ritual word Sojourner may be retained for the candidate/member.

Active Officers in the Ritual

No.OfficeFunction
1Chief RangerPresiding officer; opens and closes the Assembly, instructs the Sojourner, delivers the Charges and receives the Sojourner into the Order.
2Deputy Chief RangerPrincipal deputy; confirms orders, prepares and presents the phial, and assists the Chief Ranger.
3Trail SupervisorSenior supervisory officer; participates in the opening and comments upon the lessons of the ceremony.
4Assistant Trail SupervisorAssists with admission and challenges the Field Steward concerning the Sojourner.
5Community Liaison OfficerLeads the charitable appeal and carries the donation box to those in attendance.
6Interpretation OfficerConducts, presents and prompts the Sojourner throughout the ceremony.
7Gate RangerControls the entrance, rings the Bear Bell ceremonially and admits the Sojourner.
8Field StewardPrepares and accompanies the Sojourner from outside the Assembly and formally requests admission.
